Course Registration Guide

💡 Tip: Each university has its own registration system and schedule. Know which university you attend, then follow the instructions below!

📋 For UWI Mona Students

Registration System: SAS (Student Information System)

Steps:
  1. Go to https://www.mona.uwi.edu/online-systems
  2. Log in with your university email & M365 password
  3. Navigate to course registration
  4. Select courses and confirm enrollment
  5. Check confirmation email

⚙️ For UTech Jamaica Students

Registration System: Online Add/Drop Portal

Steps:
  1. Go to https://utech-web-srv.utech.edu.jm/AddDrop/
  2. Log in with M365 credentials (campus email)
  3. Add courses you need
  4. Drop courses you don't want
  5. Confirm all changes
⚠️ Important: Registration deadlines vary by semester and institution. Check your university's academic calendar for specific dates. Missing the deadline may result in late registration fees or course holds.

Registration Troubleshooting

Having problems with course registration? Here are common issues and solutions.

❌ Can't Log Into Registration System

UWI Mona Students:
  • Check your credentials are correct (university email + M365 password)
  • Try resetting your password at the SAS login page
  • If still blocked, email helpdesk@uwimona.edu.jm with your student ID
UTech Jamaica Students:
  • Verify your M365 account is active
  • If you don't have credentials, visit your College or Faculty computer lab
  • Lab staff can help set up or reset your access

❌ Courses Are Full or Unavailable

  • Contact your academic advisor or department chair immediately
  • Ask about waitlist options or alternative course sections
  • UWI: Faculty office may have override authority
  • UTech: Visit college registrar office

❌ Portal/Website Not Loading

UWI Mona:
  • Try clearing your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Report the issue to helpdesk@uwimona.edu.jm
  • Include: browser type, specific error message, student ID
UTech Jamaica:
  • Check the admissions page: https://www.utech.edu.jm/admissions/registration-and-enrolment/
  • Use website contact form

❌ Missed Registration Deadline

  • Act immediately: Contact registrar office ASAP
  • Request: Late registration approval
  • Be prepared: Have a valid reason (medical, emergency, etc.)
  • Note: Late registration may incur additional fees
  • Bring: Supporting documentation if applicable

✅ Can't Find Your University?

  1. Check your enrollment or acceptance letter
  2. Look at registration/tuition documents for university name
  3. Search our "All Institutions" tab for the complete list
  4. Contact Ministry of Education: moey.gov.jm
