## COURSE REGISTRATION GUIDE

### UWI Mona Registration Process

**System Used:** SAS (Student Information System)

**Steps:**
1. Log into SAS at https://www.mona.uwi.edu/online-systems
2. Use your university email and M365 credentials
3. Access course registration portal
4. Select courses and confirm enrollment

**Troubleshooting:**
- If you can't access: Email helpdesk@uwimona.edu.jm or call (876) 927-1660 ext 0
- Website issues: Report to helpdesk@uwimona.edu.jm
- SAS system problems: Contact Help Desk

---

### UTech Jamaica Registration Process

**System Used:** Online Add/Drop Portal

**Steps:**
1. Go to https://utech-web-srv.utech.edu.jm/AddDrop/
2. Log in with M365/student email credentials
3. Add or remove course modules
4. Confirm changes

**Troubleshooting:**
- Login credential issues: Contact your College or Faculty computer lab
- Can't access portal: Visit https://www.utech.edu.jm/admissions/registration-and-enrolment/

## COMMON COURSE REGISTRATION ISSUES & SOLUTIONS

### Issue: Can't Log Into Registration System

**UWI Mona:**
- Ensure you're using correct M365 credentials
- Try resetting your password at SAS login
- If still blocked: Email helpdesk@uwimona.edu.jm with your student ID

**UTech Jamaica:**
- Verify M365 account setup
- If no credentials: Visit your College or Faculty computer lab
- Lab staff can provision or reset access

---

### Issue: Courses Full or Unavailable

- **Action:** Contact your academic advisor or registrar
- **UWI:** Department Chair or Faculty office
- **UTech:** College registrar or admissions office

---

### Issue: Technical Problems with Portal

- **UWI:** Email helpdesk@uwimona.edu.jm describing the issue
- **UTech:** Check admissions page or contact through website form
- **Include:** Student ID, browser type, specific error message

---

### Issue: Registration Deadline Passed

- **Contact:** Registrar office immediately
- **Request:** Late registration approval (may have fees)
- **Bring:** Proof of valid reason (medical, emergency, etc.)

## FINDING YOUR UNIVERSITY'S RESOURCES

**Steps to Locate Your Institution's Portal/Info:**

1. **Know your university name** (check enrollment letter)
2. **Visit their official website** (see Major Universities section above)
3. **Look for:**
   - Student Portal / Login
   - Admissions / Registration
   - Academic Calendar
   - Student Services
   - Help Desk / Contact Info

4. **If stuck:**
   - Try the main phone number
   - Email admissions office
   - Visit campus registrar office in person
